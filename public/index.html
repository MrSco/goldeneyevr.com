02 · Two ways to play

Pick one at launch. Switch any time.

Stereo VR

True 3D first-person play. Look around with your head, lean around corners, and duck behind cover to hide from guards.

  • Aim with your real hand. Shots go where the barrel points, and a 3D sight shows where they'll land.
  • Hold any gun with both hands. Rifles aim along the line between your hands, with per-gun recoil and tighter spread.
  • Weapon wheel: hold A, or hold X for your left hand, to switch guns or gadgets in an instant.
  • Independent reloads: press B for your right hand, Y for your left, or both at once.
  • Raise your wrist for live health, armor, and radar. Tap the watch face for live in-game VR settings.
  • Grip gestures: reach doors and switches to use them, grab back your mines, or hip-holster your gun (work in progress).
  • Scopes magnify in either or both hands, with steadier sniper aiming up to 25x.
  • Swing either hand to fight up close: chop, punch, pistol-whip, stab or club. Sneak up on a guard and chop him.

Big virtual screen

The whole game on a cinema-sized screen floating in front of you, with no artificial motion at all.

  • Flat or curved, at any size and distance.
  • Passthrough behind the screen lets you keep your real room in view.
  • Grab the screen with both grips to reposition or carry it anywhere.
  • Hold the right stick click to jump seamlessly into stereo VR, and back.
  • The watch pause screen sits right in the room with you.
  • Menus, briefings, and cutscenes play comfortably here in both modes.

Also in the box

Weapon wheel & reloads

Hold A or X to bring up the wheel and select weapons immediately. Independent reloads on B and Y, or reload by hand from your belt with GoldenEye X (work in progress).

Wrist status & watch menu

Live health, armor, and radar arcs on your sleeve. Adjust turning speeds, comfort vignettes, and gun settings right on Bond's watch during a mission.

GoldenEye X weapons

Optional GoldenEye X models for every weapon and item, from the PP7 to the Golden Gun. Reload magazines, shells and the Magnum's speedloader by hand (work in progress, needs your own patched Perfect Dark ROM).

3D game sound

Gunfire, explosions and doors come from where they happen, through the same Steam Audio 3D sound as voice chat. A fight in the next room sounds round the corner, not through the wall.

Comfort & passthrough

Real-world passthrough behind the virtual screen, smooth-turn speed slider (45–240°/s), snap-turn vignettes, and an "Aim: no lean" option for comfort.

HD textures & updates in the headset

In-VR catalog installs the latest GoldenEye HD + AI packs, and the launcher offers each new release, no computer needed. Settings, cheats, and rules save instantly.

03 · Multiplayer · Co-op Campaign Experimental

Play the campaign in co-op. Or battle 8 players and bots.

Team up for the full solo campaign in 4-player online co-op, or jump into 8-player deathmatch across classic stages. Fill the empty slots with bots, or take on a match of bots by yourself. No IP addresses, no router setup.

  • 4-Player Co-op Campaign: Host a co-op mission from the multiplayer menu. The host picks the mission, difficulty and briefing while each player keeps their own save file and progress.
  • Built for team survival: Teammate radar tracks health and armor. Downed players can be revived instead of dying, teammates pass through each other at tight spawns, cutscenes are shared for everyone, and mission gadgets go to the whole team.
  • 8-Player Deathmatch: Host 2 to 8 players online, plus Team 3v3 and Team 4v4 matches. Spawns intelligently spread out across ammo spots so nobody starts right on top of another player.
  • Deathmatch bots: Fill the empty slots, or set 1 to 7 bots, at six difficulties from Meat to Dark. Built on Perfect Dark's simulants, they hunt by line of sight, grab guns and ammo, reload, open doors, and play Flag Tag and The Man with the Golden Gun. Anyone joining a full match takes a bot's place straight away.
  • Drop-in matches & 3D voice chat: Games stay listed after launch so players can join straight into the fight. Directional 3D voice chat features both Proximity and Couch modes.
  • Fair fights: Players gently push apart instead of pinning each other in corners. The host's Hit immunity rule lets fast guns land their hits, or keeps GoldenEye's original timing.
  • Statue and Cradle online: Battle across an expanded stage list with full weapon sets from Slappers only to the Golden Gun.
  • Warmup & pause scoreboard: 2-minute warmup lobbies, 10-second countdowns, ready requests, host kick, a No radar rule, and an in-match pause window with scores and radar arcs.
  • Same-version requirement: Multiplayer and co-op are experimental. Everyone needs v0.4.14 to play together online. The launcher offers each update right in the headset.

Online play connects headsets directly peer-to-peer when possible, and relays through Cloudflare when needed. The lobby service coordinates room codes and active player rosters only while games remain open.

05 · Install

Your mission objectives

  1. Get SideQuest in your headset

    No Developer Mode, no PC. In the Quest's browser, go to sdq.st/go. When the download finishes, open the file's three-dot menu in the browser's downloads and choose Export to install SideQuest. SideQuest's video walkthrough.

  2. Install GoldenEye VR

    Open SideQuest in the headset, search for GoldenEye VR and install it. Already set up with Developer Mode and the desktop app? Press Sideload now on the GoldenEye VR page, or drop the APK onto SideQuest.

  3. Add your ROM and play

    Copy your GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM to the headset's Download folder, over USB or from your own cloud storage in the headset's browser. Open GoldenEye VR from Library → Unknown Sources, or add it to your library from SideQuest. Press Choose ROM file..., then START.

Install with a computer Troubleshooting Controls

Bring your own game

The download contains no GoldenEye 007 game data: no ROM, graphics, sound, music, text or levels. You need a ROM of the USA (NTSC-U) cartridge that you own (.z64, .v64 or .n64, 12 MB). The app reads everything from that file on your headset. Please don't ask for ROMs.

06 · Status

Active development

GoldenEye VR is under active development. The solo campaign can be played in both Stereo VR and Big Screen modes, and 4-player online co-op and 8-player deathmatch with bots are running in early access. Expect rough edges as features evolve.

Found a bug? Open an issue with your headset model and build number. To find matches, check the live lobbies, join the Discord, or visit r/GoldenEyeVR on Reddit.
