02 · Two ways to play

Pick one at launch. Switch any time.

Stereo VR

True 3D first-person play. Look around with your head, lean around corners and duck behind cover.

  • Aim with your real hand. Shots leave the muzzle and go where the barrel points.
  • A 3D sight marks where your shot will land. The ammo counter sits on the gun.
  • Your left arm is Bond's own sleeve. Raise your wrist and look at it to open the watch.
  • Mines, the covert modem and the other gadgets are in your hand, thrown from where you hold them.
  • Smooth or snap turning, and an optional comfort vignette.

Big virtual screen

The whole game on a cinema-sized screen floating in front of you, with no artificial motion at all.

  • Flat or curved, at any size and distance.
  • Grab the screen with both grips to carry it anywhere.
  • Hold the right stick click to jump into stereo VR, and back.
  • Menus, briefings and cutscenes play here in both modes.

04 · Install

Your mission objectives

  1. Turn on Developer Mode

    Create a free organization at developers.meta.com, then switch on Developer Mode for your headset in the Meta Horizon phone app. One time only.

  2. Install with SideQuest

    Get the SideQuest Advanced Installer, plug in your Quest and allow USB debugging. Then press Sideload now on the GoldenEye VR page, or drop the APK onto SideQuest.

  3. Add your ROM and play

    Copy your GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM to the headset's Download folder. Open GoldenEye VR from Library → Unknown Sources, press Choose ROM file..., then START.

Full step-by-step guide Troubleshooting Controls

Bring your own game

The download contains no GoldenEye 007 game data: no ROM, graphics, sound, music, text or levels. You need a ROM of the USA (NTSC-U) cartridge that you own (.z64, .v64 or .n64, 12 MB). The app reads everything from that file on your headset. Please don't ask for ROMs.

05 · Status

Early release

The whole game boots, and the Dam has been played end to end in both modes. Other missions are playable but less tested, so expect rough edges. Multiplayer is on the roadmap.

Found a bug? Open an issue with the build line from the launcher, your headset, the level and what happened.
